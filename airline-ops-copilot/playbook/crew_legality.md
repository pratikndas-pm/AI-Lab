# Crew Legality & Duty Time Rules

## Flight Duty Period (FDP) Limits
- **Standard FDP**: 13 hours maximum
- **Extended FDP**: Up to 14 hours with augmented crew
- **Ultra-long haul**: Special provisions, usually 16-18 hours with relief crew

## Rest Requirements
- **Minimum rest between duties**: 12 hours
- **Post-long haul rest**: Minimum 24 hours after FDP > 10 hours
- **Local night rest**: Must include 00:00-05:00 window

## Crew Duty Time Warning Flags
- **FDP approaching limit** (within 1 hour): HIGH risk
- **Rest period compromised**: Do NOT dispatch
- **Crew member illness/fatigue report**: MUST replace crew
- **Training/certification expired**: CANNOT operate

## IROP Crew Decisions
### DO:
- Swap crew within same base if FDP allows
- Position standby crew if available
- Delay flight if crew rest would be compromised
- Cancel flight if no legal crew available

### DO NOT:
- Operate with crew exceeding FDP
- Reduce required rest periods
- Pressure crew to "stretch" limits
- Skip crew briefings to save time

## Crew Availability Check
Before any delay decision:
1. Check current FDP elapsed time
2. Add estimated delay
3. Add scheduled flight time
4. Add post-flight duties (30 min)
5. **Total must be < FDP limit**

## Escalation Triggers
- Crew requests removal due to fatigue
- FDP would exceed limit by ANY amount
- Rest facility unavailable for extended operations
- Crew shortage affecting multiple flights

## Crew Communication
- Inform crew immediately of delays
- Provide updated ETD frequently
- Coordinate meal breaks for long delays
- Arrange hotel if overnight delay

## Legal vs Operational
- **Legal**: Regulatory compliance (non-negotiable)
- **Operational**: Company policy (some flexibility)
- **When in doubt**: Treat as legal requirement
