# IROP (Irregular Operations) Basics

## Definition
IROP = Any deviation from scheduled operations affecting passengers, crew, or aircraft.

## Common IROP Causes
- **Weather**: Thunderstorms, fog, snow, wind
- **ATC (Air Traffic Control)**: Slot delays, flow control, airspace restrictions
- **Technical**: Aircraft maintenance, unscheduled repairs
- **Crew**: Duty time limits, crew unavailability, illness
- **Airport**: Ground handling, gate availability, curfew restrictions
- **Operational**: Load planning, fueling, catering delays

## Response Time Standards
- **Minor delay (0-30 min)**: Monitor, minimal intervention
- **Moderate delay (30-120 min)**: Proactive passenger communication, check connections
- **Major delay (120+ min)**: Full IROP protocol, rebooking, compensation assessment
- **Cancellation**: Immediate rebooking + accommodation/compensation

## Key Principles
1. **Passenger safety first** - Always prioritize safety over schedule
2. **Clear communication** - Inform passengers early and often
3. **Minimize passenger impact** - Protect connections, provide assistance
4. **Document everything** - Record decisions for compliance/audit
5. **Escalate when needed** - Don't hesitate to involve supervisors

## Initial Assessment Checklist
- [ ] Identify root cause
- [ ] Estimate delay duration
- [ ] Count affected passengers
- [ ] Identify special assistance passengers (wheelchair, UMNR, medical)
- [ ] Check downstream impacts (connections, crew, aircraft rotation)
- [ ] Activate appropriate response level

## Critical Decision Points
- **Below 60 min delay**: Usually no rebooking needed
- **60-180 min delay**: Offer rebooking options
- **180+ min or cancellation**: Mandatory rebooking + care provisions
- **Overnight delay**: Hotel accommodation required
