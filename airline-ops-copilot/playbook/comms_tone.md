# Communication Tone & Templates

## Core Communication Principles
1. **Empathy first**: Acknowledge inconvenience
2. **Clarity**: Simple language, avoid jargon
3. **Proactive**: Tell them before they ask
4. **Honest**: Don't overpromise
5. **Actionable**: Clear next steps

## Tone Guidelines
### DO:
- Use "we apologize" (not "sorry for the inconvenience")
- Be specific: "Your new flight departs at 14:30" (not "later today")
- Acknowledge impact: "We understand this disrupts your plans"
- Offer solutions: "Here's what we've arranged..."
- Use active voice: "We've rebooked you" (not "You have been rebooked")

### DO NOT:
- Blame others (ATC, weather, other airlines)
- Use airline jargon (IROP, MCT, PAX, PNR)
- Over-apologize (one sincere apology is enough)
- Make excuses
- Use robotic language ("as per policy...")

## Message Structure
1. **Apology** (brief, sincere)
2. **Situation** (what happened)
3. **Impact** (how it affects them)
4. **Solution** (what we've done)
5. **Next steps** (what they should do)
6. **Contact** (how to reach us)

## Sample Templates

### Delay Notification (Passenger)
```
We apologize for the delay to flight [XX123] to [DESTINATION].

Due to [REASON], your flight will now depart at [NEW TIME], approximately [X hours] later than scheduled.

We've kept your existing booking. Please arrive at gate [XX] by [BOARDING TIME].

If you need assistance or wish to explore alternative flights, please visit our service desk at [LOCATION] or call [NUMBER].

Thank you for your patience.
```

### Misconnection - Auto Rebooked
```
We apologize - due to the delay, you'll miss your connection in [CITY].

We've automatically rebooked you on flight [XX456] departing at [TIME]. Your new arrival time in [DESTINATION] is [TIME].

Your baggage will be transferred to the new flight.

Please proceed to gate [XX]. Our team will assist you.

Ref: [PNR/BOOKING NUMBER]
```

### Cancellation Notice
```
We sincerely apologize - flight [XX123] to [DESTINATION] has been cancelled due to [REASON].

We've rebooked you on the next available flight [XX456] on [DATE] at [TIME].

You're entitled to:
- Rebooking on alternative flight (arranged)
- Meal voucher (collect at service desk)
- Hotel accommodation (if overnight) - details below

Please visit our service desk at [LOCATION] for immediate assistance.

Hotel: [NAME], [ADDRESS] - shuttle departs every 30 min
Meals: Voucher valid at [RESTAURANTS]

We're here to help: [PHONE] / [EMAIL]
```

### Internal Agent Script
```
SITUATION: Flight XX123 delayed 3h, ATC restrictions

PASSENGER IMPACT: 18 misconnects at DXB

ACTIONS TAKEN:
- Auto-rebooked 16 passengers on XX456 (departs 2h after arrival)
- 2 UMNR require manual handling (see escalation)
- Baggage retag in progress

NEXT STEPS:
- Monitor connection feasibility
- Coordinate with DXB station for UMNR handling
- Issue meal vouchers if delay exceeds 3h
- Update passengers via SMS

CONTACT: Duty Manager [NAME] ext [XXXX]
```

## Language Customization
- **Formal markets** (GCC, Asia): More formal, respectful tone
- **Casual markets** (US, Australia): Friendly, conversational
- **Emergency situations**: Clear, directive, calm

## Special Passenger Communication
- **UMNR**: Speak to child + parent/guardian (separate messages)
- **Wheelchair**: Confirm assistance arranged
- **VIP**: Private, discreet communication
- **Groups**: Single message to group leader + individual confirmations

## Escalation Communication
When escalating to manager:
```
ESCALATION REQUEST

Flight: [XX123]
Reason: [Brief description]
Passenger(s): [Count + special categories]
Current status: [What's been done]
Decision needed: [Specific ask]
Urgency: [High/Medium]

Agent: [NAME/ID]
Time: [TIMESTAMP]
```

## Multi-Channel Consistency
- SMS: Brief, actionable, include reference number
- Email: Detailed, includes all info + next steps
- Gate announcement: Clear, repeated 2-3 times
- App notification: Short, link to details
- Phone call: Empathetic, two-way conversation

## Crisis Communication (Serious Delays/Incidents)
- Acknowledge severity
- Commit to updates every [X hours]
- Provide dedicated helpline
- Assign single point of contact
- Document all communication
