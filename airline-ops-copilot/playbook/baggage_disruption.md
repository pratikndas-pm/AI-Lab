# Baggage Disruption Handling

## Types of Baggage Issues
1. **Mishandled**: Arrived on wrong flight/destination
2. **Delayed**: Still in system, not on passenger's flight
3. **Damaged**: Physical damage during handling
4. **Lost**: Cannot be located after 5 days (domestic) / 21 days (international)
5. **Pilferage**: Items missing from baggage

## Immediate Actions
- Issue PIR (Property Irregularity Report) number
- Provide passenger with interim expense allowance (if applicable)
- Trace bag in WorldTracer system
- Estimate delivery time frame
- Arrange delivery to passenger address

## Misconnection Baggage Protocol
If passenger misses connection:
1. **Pull bag from original connection flight** (if possible)
2. **Retag bag** with new flight routing
3. **Verify bag loaded** on new flight
4. **Communicate to passenger**: "Your bag will arrive with you" or "Bag will follow on next flight"

## Priority Baggage
- **Medical equipment**: URGENT - hand-deliver if possible
- **Medication**: URGENT - arrange immediate delivery
- **Wheelchair**: CRITICAL - coordinate with special assistance
- **Infant items**: HIGH - expedite delivery

## Compensation Guidelines (Placeholder)
- **Delayed bag (domestic)**: Interim expenses up to $50/day
- **Delayed bag (international)**: Per Montreal Convention limits
- **Damaged bag**: Repair or replacement value
- **Lost bag**: Compensation per airline policy + liability limits

## Communication Templates
### Bag will arrive on next flight:
"Your checked baggage will arrive on flight [XX123] at [TIME]. We'll deliver it to [ADDRESS] within 4 hours of arrival."

### Bag delayed unknown:
"We're actively tracing your baggage. You'll receive updates via SMS every 6 hours. PIR number: [XXXXX]."

## DO:
- Issue PIR immediately
- Provide passenger with essentials kit (if available)
- Set clear delivery expectations
- Keep passenger updated every 6-24 hours

## DO NOT:
- Promise delivery time you can't guarantee
- Offer compensation amounts without authorization
- Blame other airlines/handlers (even if true)
- Close PIR before passenger confirms receipt
