# Customer Tiers & Priority Handling

## Frequent Flyer Tiers
### Platinum (Top Tier)
- Priority rebooking (first in queue)
- Complimentary lounge access during delays
- Proactive SMS/email updates
- Waive change fees automatically
- Offer alternative airports/routes
- Consider upgrade if available

### Gold (Mid Tier)
- Priority rebooking (after Platinum)
- Lounge access voucher if delay > 3 hours
- Proactive communication
- Waive change fees
- Offer alternatives

### Silver (Entry Tier)
- Standard rebooking priority
- Standard communication
- May waive fees depending on circumstances
- Offer alternatives if available

### Standard (No Status)
- Standard rebooking
- Standard communication
- Standard policies apply

## Special Assistance Categories
### UMNR (Unaccompanied Minor)
- **Always priority**
- Must be supervised at all times
- Cannot be left alone in airport
- Contact parent/guardian immediately on delay
- Assign airline staff escort if connection missed
- **NEVER** put on flight without confirmed supervision at destination

### Wheelchair (WCHR, WCHS, WCHC)
- Priority boarding and deplaning
- Ensure wheelchair available at destination
- If connection missed, arrange assistance at accommodation
- Coordinate with special services team
- Cannot leave passenger without mobility aid

### Medical (MEDA)
- Assess urgency of travel
- Consult with medical team if delay critical
- Priority rebooking if time-sensitive
- Ensure medical equipment/medication accessible
- Consider alternative airports near medical facilities

### Pregnant Passengers
- Comfort considerations (seating, rest)
- Priority if late-term (28+ weeks)
- Medical clearance check for long delays
- Offer alternatives if uncomfortable with delay

## VIP / Media
- Government officials
- Celebrities
- Media crew
- Corporate executives (major accounts)

**Handling**: Discreet, professional, no special announcements, lounge access, private communication, escalate to Duty Manager

## Family with Infants
- Priority for seat changes (keep together)
- Offer bassinet if available
- Provide infant care items if available
- Accommodate feeding/diaper change needs

## Groups
- Keep together when possible
- Single point of contact
- Coordinate rebooking as unit
- May split if no other option, but document

## Priority Order for Rebooking (When Limited Seats)
1. UMNR + wheelchair + medical
2. Platinum frequent flyers
3. Family with infants
4. Gold frequent flyers
5. Pregnant passengers
6. Silver frequent flyers
7. Standard passengers (FIFO - First In, First Out)

## Communication by Tier
- **Platinum/Gold**: Personal call or SMS
- **Silver**: SMS + email
- **Standard**: Email + gate announcements
- **Special assistance**: Always personal contact + guardian notification

## DO NOT:
- Announce tier status publicly (privacy)
- Discriminate based on tier (basic rights apply to all)
- Promise benefits not in policy
- Ignore standard passengers while helping VIPs
